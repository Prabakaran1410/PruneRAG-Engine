ACME CLOUD SECURITY POLICY | CONTROL REGISTER

Control ACME-IAM-042
Privileged access must use phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication. The control owner is Identity Engineering. Evidence must be retained for 365 days. Exceptions require approval from the Chief Information Security Officer.

Control ACME-LOG-117
Audit logs for production systems must be immutable, time-synchronized, and retained for 400 days. The logging owner is Security Operations. The exact control identifier is ACME-LOG-117.

Control ACME-BCP-009
Critical services must have a recovery time objective of four hours and a recovery point objective of one hour. The continuity owner is Site Reliability Engineering.
